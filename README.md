 Project Overview
This project features an interactive Sales Dashboard created to visualize and analyze sales performance data from January 2014 to December 2017. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of key performance indicators (KPIs), trends, and breakdowns by segment, category, and time period.

     Key Metrics (KPIs)
The dashboard highlights the following aggregate figures for the selected period:

Total Sales: $2.25M

Total Profit: $278.51K

Total Quantity Sold: 37K

Date Range: 17-01-2014 to 16-12-2017

     Visualizations Included
1. Sales and Profit by Year
Type: Combination Chart (Bar & Line)

Insight: Visualizes the upward trend of sales (bars) and profit (line) over the four-year period from 2014 to 2017.

2. Sales and Profit by Segment
Type: Pie Chart

Segments:

Consumer: (Highest share ~50.44%)

Corporate: (~30.93%)

Home Office: (~18.63%)

3. Profit by Category
Type: Donut Chart

Categories:

Technology: (Highest profit share ~51.29%)

Office Supplies: (~42.77%)

Furniture: (~5.94%)

4. Sales by Year and Quarter (QTR)
Type: Clustered Column Chart

Details: Breaks down sales performance by Quarter (Qtr 1 - Qtr 4) for each year, helping identify seasonal trends.

5. Subcategory Sales, Profit & Profit Margin
Type: Detailed Table

Data Points: Lists top subcategories such as Accessories, Appliances, Art, and Binders, showing:

Sum of Sales

Sum of Profit

Profit Margin %
